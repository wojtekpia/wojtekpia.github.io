Home Assistant and Nest
Privacy policy

A private link between my house and its thermostats

This is a personal app. It lets the Home Assistant server in my home read and control the Nest thermostats in my home, using Google’s Device Access API. It isn’t a product, and nobody else uses it.

Read the privacy policy
What it does
Reads temperature, humidity, mode, and target settings from my thermostats, and changes the heating and cooling mode and target temperature when my home automations ask it to.
Where the data goes
From Google to a Home Assistant server in my house. It isn’t copied to any other service, and it isn’t used for advertising, analytics, or anything else.
Who uses it
One person: the owner of the Google account that the thermostats belong to.
